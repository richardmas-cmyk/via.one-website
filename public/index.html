One Platform Instead of Three Different Shops

Families who send money home also top up phones and need a card they can use. Via One puts all of it on one account, one ledger and one relationship — for the store, the partner and the customer.

  • Money to Mexico – bank deposit to any CLABE account, usually in minutes
  • Mobile top-ups – Mexico's major networks plus international airtime
  • Prepaid cards – issue, load and manage Visa and Mastercard cards
  • One integration – store POS, WhatsApp and API on the same platform

Built for Stores and the People They Serve

From a single corner store to a national distributor, Via One handles the money movement, receipts and reporting so you can focus on your customers.

Services

What You Can Do With Via One

Six products on one platform, available in store, on WhatsApp and through our API.

Money Transfers to Mexico

Customers send from a partner store, by card, or on WhatsApp. Funds arrive as a bank deposit to any Mexican CLABE account, usually within minutes, with a receipt for both sides.

SPEI bank deposits

Mobile Top-Ups

Real-time airtime and data for Telcel, Movistar, AT&T, Bait and international networks — sold at the counter, on WhatsApp, or wholesale through our API.

Mexico + international

Prepaid & Virtual Cards

Card programs for partners: issue virtual cards one at a time or in bulk, load them, and manage them from a web portal. Ready for Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Visa & Mastercard

WhatsApp Wallet

Customers check their balance, send money, top up phones and load cards in a WhatsApp chat — no app to download, in English or Spanish.

English & Spanish

Store Point of Sale

A browser-based till for retail partners: cash in, transfers, top-ups and card loads, with daily statements and settlement reports for the owner.

Web POS

Partner API

Distributors and fintechs connect once for top-ups, payouts and cards, with real-time webhooks, reporting and a partner dashboard.
